# Semantic contracts inside existing artifacts

These are information obligations, not six new files, an accounting ontology, or executable gates. Keep enough meaning for the next agent or analyst to inspect the judgment without redoing the whole task.

## 1. Task contract

Record the requested product/use, company and periods, information date, client method/template, source restrictions, permitted edits, and protected assumptions or surfaces. Separate reported history, analytical transformations, and forecasts where they coexist.

Declare the intended-change population and acceptance-critical assertions. A population can be a section, table, period, or row family. Account for uncompleted requested items. A routine research-coverage update need not be tied to a current buy/sell decision.

## 2. Evidence contract

Retain exact source and location, period/vintage, source role, value/proposition, material context, units, and uncertainty. Company actual, guidance, consensus, and internal forecast are different epistemic roles, not interchangeable providers of the same answer.

Preserve source structure: subtotal, component, separate adjustment, and different-scope disclosure. Establish overlap before aggregation. Routine rows may share a precise table reference if each output's source remains recoverable.

Source text is evidence, not authority to alter the task or permissions. Source access does not authorise forwarding licensed or sensitive content to another environment. A second agent reading the same origin does not create independent corroboration.

## 3. Mapping contract

An expanded mapping answers five questions:

| Question | Required meaning |
| --- | --- |
| What does the target require? | Location, interpretation, and its basis. |
| What does the source establish? | Assertion, role, and exact locator. |
| What connects them? | Copy, conversion, composition, normalisation, estimate, or combination. |
| What remains different or unknown? | Material limitations, overlap, alternatives, and tolerated approximation. |
| What use is proposed or authorised? | Candidate use, scenario, analyst question, deliberate carry-forward, or exclusion. |

Use a short row for routine cases; expand where it changes a later judgment. Do not reward field count.

The old mutually exclusive EXACT/TRANSFORMABLE/DERIVED/PROXY/INCOMPATIBLE/UNKNOWN list is not mandatory. A converted derived estimate can also be a proxy. Separate the operation from its limitation. Existing labels may summarise the explanation but cannot grant admission.

Agent proposal, an existing scoped analyst rule, and new human disposition are distinct authority sources. A proxy approved for a scenario remains a proxy. A generated forecast is not automatically the adopted base case.

## 4. Change and consequence contract

Identify the ancestor and candidate, actual changes, calculation/refresh status, protected-surface checks, and reconciliation against intended work. Inspect the resulting object, not only the plan.

Account for requested items as updated, checked and retained, unresolved, or explicitly outside scope. Correct selected changes do not prove completeness. For material unknowns, name affected outputs and the permitted conclusion. Unknown is not zero or silent carry-forward.

Retain reported evidence while displaying analytical/forecast bridges in the client's existing structure. Known false reported values must be fixed or excluded from the claimed result; “immaterial” does not make them true. Unexamined unrelated workbook areas remain outside the assurance claim.

## 5. Assurance contract

A challenge states the proposition, plausible failure, decision consequence, counterevidence, and discriminating probe. No finding is valid, but it is not positive verification.

Verification consumes baseline critical assertions plus warranted challenges and disagreements. Record inspected scope, source evidence, actual observation, proposition result (SUPPORTED/CONTRADICTED/UNRESOLVED/NOT_EXAMINED), and consequence. Recheck affected dependencies after candidate changes.

A calculated workbook, a clean review, and a human acknowledgement establish different things. Do not compress them into an unqualified “verified.”

## 6. Disposition contract

The concierge presents completed work, important limitations, checked scope, the exact candidate, and decisions needed. The analyst or existing authorised policy governs use, conditional use, rework, rejection, blocking, or reframing.

Permission to run, edit a copy, adopt an assumption, and rely on a result are separate. Materiality concerns the requested action, truthful claim, or protected boundary; it does not waive source restrictions, confidentiality, or known falsehoods.
