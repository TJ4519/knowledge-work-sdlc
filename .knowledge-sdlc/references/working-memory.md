# Document-based semantic working memory

Use this working ontology to preserve each consequential statement's role,
scope, evidence and relationship to the work. It supports production, current
recovery, historical questions and scoped correction in the existing project
documents, alongside native notes and search.

## One owner, recoverable references

The source snapshot owns retained source content; the evidence map owns its
interpretation and mapping; the candidate owns the resulting analysis; the
decision owns the human act. The run record owns plan, outcomes and live pointers.
Do not duplicate these into a master summary or global claims register.

For a consequential item, make the following recoverable in those owners:

| Question | Minimum useful content |
| --- | --- |
| What is it? | Proposition, assumption or mapping with a stable local heading/anchor or an equally exact locator. |
| What does it cover? | Subject, period, definition, scenario and intended use where they distinguish meanings. |
| What role does it play? | Source report, calculation, interpretation, analyst assumption or human decision; roles are not truth scores. |
| What supports it? | Exact source revision/passage, derivation or decision reference. |
| When did it apply? | Period described, evidence availability and recorded time where observed; unknown times remain unknown. |
| Where is it used? | Actual candidate paragraph, table, chart, model range or other material consumer. |
| What changed? | Scoped predecessor/successor relationship and the documented reason. |
| What was checked? | Exact target, basis, observation, result and remaining coverage. |

Use ordinary Markdown headings such as `### revenue-q2` and a relative reference
like `candidate-r2.md#revenue-q2`. Bind the containing revision/hash in the existing
handoff. For native work use the exact candidate revision plus sheet/range,
slide/object or other address. A link label is not evidence that its target was
read. Old records without anchors use a quoted passage or exact existing
location; missing metadata must not make usable historical evidence invalid.

An item key may persist between revisions to connect the same question. Its
meaning is supplied by each revision, not frozen forever by its name. If a
revision changes the definition or scope, state that explicitly. Never redirect
an old targeted file/anchor to new bytes. Preserve immutable revisions; a sparse
source-to-use link is preferable to copying the same explanation everywhere.
Routine homogeneous rows may share a precise table/range mapping. Expand only
where a distinction changes a subsequent judgment. No per-cell paperwork quota.

## Write when the choice matters

Before a consequential assignment, establish what the target requires, what the
source establishes, and the conversion, composition or estimate connecting them.
Retain a short explanation in its existing owner. Apply it to the actual output,
then inspect the result. The record must be consumed during production/checking,
not just attached after delivery.

Maintain only material relationships: source supports interpretation; an
assumption or method contributes to a result; a result is used in a conclusion;
a decision permits a stated use; a check examines a particular target. These
relations have different effects. A source can support a calculation without
authorising its use. Human acceptance does not verify a fact. A repeated opinion
is not independent corroboration.

At a material revision, preserve the earlier basis and link a successor for the
changed scope. An estimate of 100 becoming a reported actual of 100 is a semantic
change despite identical values. Conversely, a new management-guidance scenario
does not supersede the analyst's scenario merely because it is newer. Establish
what was revised, reconsidered and retained, unresolved, or outside the work.

Do not repair a financial/model relationship by forcing all representations to
agree. A documented difference can be the right result. Reasonable estimates
remain legitimate when their method, scope and uncertainty are explicit.

## Read for the present question

Current continuation uses `kw-prime`: selected initiative, current run, meaning,
decisions and required live outputs. Historical inquiry uses `kw-recall` only
when the question needs earlier records. Keep recall within the selected
initiative and its authorised source bindings.

For historical questions, separate what evidence existed from what the retained
record shows was used. Report missing rationale; do not fabricate or backdate
one. Later evidence may refute an earlier position without changing the account
of what was known then. Similarity and a recent file timestamp are discovery
cues, not proof of applicability or supersession.

Before present reuse, inspect the relevant subject, definition, period, scenario,
method and authority. A check must still apply to both its target and its basis.
A hash identifies bytes; it does not establish professional correctness.

## Correction must reach its consumers

Identify the exact changed item and follow its recorded input revisions and
observed native/source-to-claim relationships. Distinguish observed dependants
from suspected ones. When an omitted edge is plausible, inspect the bounded
nearby model/document surface; record the newly observed relation or the gap.
Do not infer an edge from chronology or thematic similarity, and do not claim
complete propagation from an incomplete map.

For each material consumer, state whether it needs revision, a labelled bridge,
preserved disagreement, deliberate retention or further evidence. Return stale
work to its existing producer. The correction is not done when the ledger changes:
inspect the successor calculation, chart and written claim as applicable.
Preserve unaffected history and parallel scenarios. Missing forecast inputs must
not erase independently known historical data. Recheck only the affected support
and representative preserved behaviour; carry old checks forward narrowly.

The existing orchestration, correction, protected-promotion and fresh-challenge
requirements retain their authority. This convention adds no gate and waives no
required review. A harmless permitted correction does not need another approval
merely because a new link was recorded.

## Record compatibility

All retained material stays project-owned. Closed runs, source snapshots and
historical decisions are not rewritten to install this convention. Missing
history narrows an answer; no migration fabricates it. New normative rules and
reusable lessons retain their existing admission/selection routes.
