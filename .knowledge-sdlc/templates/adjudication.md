# Adjudication

## Inputs
Exact candidate and non-empty challenge revisions, all targeting the same candidate.

## Reproduction ledger
For each challenge: reproduced, partially reproduced, not reproduced, or unresolved; cite evidence and method.

## Disagreements and false positives
Explain why claims were merged, narrowed, downgraded, dropped, or retained.

## Surviving contradiction
State consequences, required correction, and any human judgment that remains.

## Verdict boundary
The adjudication informs disposition. It cannot accept, reject, amend, or promote the candidate.
