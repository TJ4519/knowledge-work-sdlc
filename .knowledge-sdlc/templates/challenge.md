# Independent challenge

## Independence receipt
Candidate revision, challenger agent/model receipt, excluded producer context, and fresh-session identity.

## Challenge target
One exact candidate revision and hash.

## Search method and coverage
Concrete failure class, evidence examined, comparisons run, and stopping boundary.

## Findings
For each finding:
- proposition challenged
- retained evidence and exact location
- failure consequence
- reproduce/recheck handle
- severity or materiality rationale
- proposed resolution

## False positives rejected
Record plausible objections tested and not supported.

## Unresolved contradiction
State what evidence or human judgment is still required.

## Boundary
A challenge is a claim to be adjudicated, not authority or a vote.
