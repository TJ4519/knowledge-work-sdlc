# Re-derivation

## Reproduction boundary
Exact meaning, evidence, method/work order, accepted change, model receipt, and excluded original-producer context.

## Steps performed
Sufficient ordered method for another fresh agent to repeat.

## Result comparison
Original/accepted result versus fresh result, matched dimensions, discrepancies, and explanations.

## Source and payload checks
Hashes, definitions, periods, protected scope, and validation results.

## Claim
Reproduced, partially reproduced, not reproduced, or blocked by missing evidence.

## Boundary
Re-derivation evidence informs the human; it does not retroactively grant authority.
