# Reconciliation

## Changed record
Exact correction, decision, or promotion that triggered reconciliation.

## Dependency coverage
Explicit dependants inspected, missing graph coverage, and unrelated work checked as unaffected.

## Deviations and downstream consequences
For each affected object: recorded assumption, current reality, evidence, severity, and recommendation.

## Consumer checks
Identify the actual model range, chart or claim and its predecessor/successor
basis. Distinguish observed dependency, suspected impact and inspected no-impact.
For each material consumer: revise, bridge, preserve disagreement, retain with
reason, or investigate. Name the producer and required successor observation;
do not call a proposed change applied. Preserve unrelated history and scenarios.

## Actions by ownership
- regenerable agent artefacts to supersede
- human-approved artefacts requiring proposed amendment
- protected artefacts requiring a future candidate/promotion
- immutable sources retained unchanged

## Vocabulary or method drift
Flag for the appropriate standing-method owner; do not silently rewrite it.
