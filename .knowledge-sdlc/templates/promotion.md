# Promotion receipt

## Authority and payload
Candidate descriptor, native payload, human Use decision, protected target, and intended use.

## Integrity
Pre-promotion target hash, payload hash, temporary validation, post-promotion hash, and media type.

## Controlled action
Timestamp, bound host/facility, observed transactional or atomic replacement
guarantee, result, and rollback handle.

## Post-promotion validation
Commands/checks and results.

## Boundary
The receipt proves custody and exact-byte relations. It does not prove professional correctness.
