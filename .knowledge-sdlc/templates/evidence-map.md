# Evidence map

## Commission boundary
Settled meaning revision, protected object, search depth, and coverage limits.

## Evidence ledger
For every material fact or proposed change:
- claim/change identifier
- source snapshot revision and exact location
- direct fact versus derived inference
- unit, period, scope, definition, and comparability
- transformation or calculation
- conflicts and unresolved gaps

## Search coverage
Sources examined, sources unavailable, dependency/native-artifact surfaces inspected, and stopping rationale.

## Rival routes
Link live and eliminated alternatives with evidence.

## Consumer
Work order, producer, challengers, adjudicator, reconciler, and re-derivation auditor.
