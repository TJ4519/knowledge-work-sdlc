# Rival route

## Premise
State the materially different interpretation or method without caricature.

## Required evidence
Name the evidence that would support or eliminate it.

## Consequence
State how the route changes source use, method, protected scope, candidate, or conclusion.

## Elimination and reopen conditions
Record what closes the route and what later evidence would reopen it.

## Status
Live, eliminated, or unresolved. Confidence alone does not eliminate a route.
