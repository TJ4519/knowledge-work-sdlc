# Example expert extension: sell-side research vocabulary

This is an example import bundle, not part of the generic core and not an
automatically selected profile.

## Owner and purpose

- Example owner: research-method lead
- Purpose: adapt generic commission language to sell-side research artifacts
- Allowed effect: vocabulary and required-field prompts for a bound run
- Prohibited effect: no source, valuation, model, protected-write, or human
  authority; no change to the generic initiative/run grammar

## Vocabulary

| Generic term | Bound extension term |
|---|---|
| commissioner | analyst |
| protected artefact | workbook |
| professional research | research note |
| subject | issuer |
| period or version | reporting period or scenario |

## Method cues

- Workbook update: issuer, period, protected/editable scope, scenario, source
  basis, house template and intended use.
- Source interpretation: issuer, metric, definition, basis, historical
  treatment and intended use.
- Research question: issuer, question, horizon, evidence/claim scope, template
  and intended use.
- Thesis challenge: issuer, candidate revision, challenge/evidence scope and
  decision use.

The curation process should admit, narrow, amend, or reject this example exactly
as it would a lead-supplied bundle.
